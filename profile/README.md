# STRATAON

**Industrial automation, monitoring, power management and security solutions.**

STRATAON develops professional hardware and software solutions for automation,
environmental monitoring, power management and electronic security.

Our products are designed for integration with corporate monitoring, automation
and security platforms through open and industry-standard protocols.

## Products

- **miniPLC** — Industrial automation and I/O controller
- **miniENV** — Environmental monitoring and automation
- **sentiaMAN** — Monitoring and management platform
- **sentiaPRO** — Environmental monitoring and rack access control for data centers
- **sentiaLOK** — Distributed access control and I/O monitoring platform
- **smartPDU** — Intelligent power distribution and remote management

## Integration & Developer Resources
